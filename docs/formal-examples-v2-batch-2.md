## v2-ex-015

**Target sense**

* 먹다 sense 2 — take medicine

**Korean**  
감기약을 하루에 세 번 먹어요.

**English**  
I take cold medicine three times a day.

**Chinese**  
我一天吃三次感冒药。

**Japanese**  
風邪薬を一日に3回飲みます。

**Approved lexical links**

* 먹어요 → 먹다 sense 2

**Important translation note**

* Korean naturally uses `약을 먹다`.
* Japanese naturally uses `薬を飲む`.
* Do not mechanically translate the Korean verb into Japanese.

**Register:** 해요체 / neutral polite  
**Provenance:** original
