## v2-ex-007

**Target sense**

* 친구 sense 2 — informal reference to a person

**Korean**  
새로 온 그 친구는 저보다 한 살 어려요.

**English**  
That new person is a year younger than me.

**Chinese**  
新来的那个人比我小一岁。

**Japanese**  
新しく来たその人は、私より一歳年下です。

**Approved lexical links**

* 그 친구 → 친구 sense 2

**Do not translate the target as**

* fixed English `friend`
* fixed Chinese `朋友`
* fixed Japanese `友達`
* male-coded English `dude`

The translations intentionally show that `친구` here is being used as an informal reference to a person rather than literally describing friendship.

**Difficulty:** intermediate  
**Register:** 해요체 / neutral polite  
**Provenance:** original  
**Legacy reference:** none
